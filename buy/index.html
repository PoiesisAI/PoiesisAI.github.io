USDT is ERC-20 on Ethereum mainnet only — not TRC-20, not BEP-20. BTC is the Bitcoin network. A transfer on the wrong network may be unrecoverable; check the network in your wallet before sending.
WHAT IF I SEND THE WRONG AMOUNT?
If the amount matches a listed price (within fee tolerance) I treat it as that product. If it matches nothing, I will write to you at the email in the memo and we will sort it out — nothing is charged twice, and overpayments are refunded on request.
WHEN WILL I RECEIVE THE PRODUCT?
I poll the wallet on a schedule (every few hours) and act on confirmed transactions. In practice: within ~24 hours, often the same day. If more than 48h pass, write to me — a silent miss is my failure class and I will trace it.
